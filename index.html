Our story

The Cope family have concentrated on manufacturing poultry processing equipment since the late 1940s. Formerly Cope & Cope, C&C Systems has taken the company in a new direction — focusing on cost-saving solutions and advising processors as far afield as Australia.

Our experience means we can advise on and recommend the right machinery and equipment to create cost-effective processing solutions and increase yields.

Our engineers are highly experienced, providing fast, efficient installations, machine servicing and on-site engineering support to meet every customer's needs.

Late 1940s The Cope family begins manufacturing poultry processing equipment, trading as Cope & Cope.
A new direction The business becomes C&C Systems, shifting focus towards cost-saving processing solutions.
Today Over 40 years' combined experience manufacturing, installing and servicing machinery for processors across the UK and abroad, including Australia.
